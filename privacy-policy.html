Privacy Policy

Simple Meme, on iOS and on the web. Last updated 2026-10-29.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising, and this app does not set cookies. The only thing kept in your browser is your own saved memes, stored locally so they are still there when you come back — plus, only if you choose to add one, your own Anthropic API key for the optional caption-ideas feature below. The iOS app carries an Apple privacy manifest declaring no tracking and no collected data types, which matches what the app actually does on both platforms: nothing is collected.

Where your pictures go

Nowhere but your own device. Editing, exporting and saving a project all happen locally — in this browser's own storage on the web, and in the app's own storage on iOS. No server of ours ever receives your photos or videos, because there is no server here that could.

The camera

On iOS, "Be the meme", "Put me in the meme" and "Swap your face" use the camera, and only after you tap one and allow it. The live picture is read on your phone — finding a face, a body or an outline happens on-device with Apple's Vision framework — and it is never recorded or sent anywhere. A photo you take stays on your device: in your meme, or in "My poses" if you choose to save it.

Face data

"Swap your face" and "Talk" find the eyes, nose, mouth and outline of a face in a photo, using Apple's Vision framework on your device, so they can put your face, eyes or mouth into a meme or open a mouth in time with your voice. Those face positions are used only for that one edit, at the moment you make it: they are not stored, not used to identify anyone, not used for advertising or analytics, and never leave your device or reach us or anyone else. The picture that results is saved only where you save it — in your meme, on your device.

The microphone

On iOS, "Record your voice" uses the microphone, only after you tap it and allow it, to make a meme talk. The recording is saved in your meme on your device and goes nowhere else; it leaves your device only if you export the meme as a video and share it yourself.

Memes made with the app's maker's face

The "My face memes" in the app are classic meme scenes made with the face of its maker, Lipai Wang, and are included with his permission. They are part of the app, not anyone's personal data, and you may use them in the memes you make.

Caption ideas: the one feature that uses the network

The optional "Caption ideas" button sends the words you type describing your meme — never your picture — to Anthropic's API, so it can suggest captions. It works only with your own Anthropic API key, which you provide yourself and which is stored only on your device: in this browser's local storage on the web, or the iOS Keychain in the app. Until you add a key, this feature does nothing and nothing is sent anywhere.

Remix links

Sharing a remix link sends the layout and captions of your meme, never the photo. Whoever opens the link gets the structure of your joke with empty picture slots, not the picture itself.

If this editor is embedded in another site

This editor can be placed inside another website's own page. When that happens, the finished meme can be sent only to a web address that site declared up front — never to an unknown destination, and never to a server of ours. That website decides when to ask for the meme; this app only enforces which address it is allowed to send it to.

In-app purchases

The iOS app offers Simple Meme Pro — a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase — handled entirely by Apple's App Store using StoreKit: Apple processes the payment, we never see your card or payment details, and we keep no purchase records of our own — the app asks StoreKit on your device what you have bought. A few free tries of Pro features are counted in a small file on your device, never sent anywhere. Nothing that is free is behind Pro, and the web app sells nothing.

Children

Simple Meme Maker is not directed at children under 13. It does not matter who is using it, though — as described above, we collect no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. There is no separate changelog to check — the date is the whole signal.
