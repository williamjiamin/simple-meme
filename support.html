Support

Email williamjiaminus@gmail.com and Lipai Wang, who made the app, will answer. Please say which device and iOS version you are using.

Where are my memes saved?

On your device only, in the app's own storage. Nothing is synced to a server, so deleting the app deletes your saved projects — export anything you want to keep to Photos first.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the editor, open More › Simple Meme Pro and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple account you bought it with.

How do I cancel Simple Meme Pro?

In Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.

A remix link will not open

Remix links open in the Simple Meme app. Install it from the App Store, then tap the link again.

Caption ideas do nothing

That feature only works once you paste your own Anthropic API key into the caption-ideas panel. Without one, nothing is sent anywhere.
